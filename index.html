19:30 — Check in · Hotel Drip&Drop · Myeongdong
Drop bags. Freshen up. Myeongdong street food is right outside — tteokbokki, hotteok, corn dogs everywhere. Good for a quick snack while you settle.
Sidequest: Myeongdong is THE duty-free street in Seoul. Laneige, Innisfree, Sulwhasoo all have flagship stores within 2 min walk. Good for K-beauty skincare if that's your thing.
20:00 — Born & Bred · Hanwoo Omakase · BOOKED ✓
📍 1 Majang-ro 42-gil, Seongdong-gu · Near Majang Station, Line 5 Exit 3
Getting there from hotel: Myeongdong Stn (Line 4) → transfer at Dongdaemun History & Culture (Line 5) → Majang Stn (~25 min total). Or taxi ~15 min, ~₩12,000.
7 seats only. Chef cooks every course at the counter. Grade 9 BMS 1++ female cattle. Binchotan charcoal. 14–19 courses: tartare, tataki, tenderloin, striploin, outside skirt, beef katsu. Runs ~2–2.5h. Done by ~22:30. ₩380,000/person.
22:30 — Siloam Bulgama Sauna · Post-dinner jjimjilbang
📍 253 Seosomun-ro, Jung-gu · Near Seoul Station (Line 1/4)
Getting there from Born & Bred: Majang Stn Line 5 → Seoul Station (~20 min). Or taxi ~15 min, ~₩12,000.
Open 24 hours. 6 floors of saunas: charcoal room, clay room, Himalayan salt, ice room, multiple hot pool temperatures. Gender-separated bathing floors (go in naked — totally normal, nobody stares). Get the seshin body scrub (세신, ~₩25,000 add-on) — an attendant uses a coarse mitt to scrub off your entire outer layer of dead skin. Sounds brutal, feels incredible, skin will be silky for days. Eat hard-boiled eggs and sikhye (sweet rice drink) after — the jjimjilbang ritual. Entry ₩12,000–15,000. Towel + pyjamas included.
Back to hotel: Seoul Station → Line 4 → Myeongdong (2 stops, ~5 min). Late trains run until 00:30 KST.
📋 Itinerary — May 17 (Sun) · LEAVE HOTEL BY 08:45
08:45 — Leave hotel → Gyeongbokgung Palace
📍 161 Sajik-ro, Jongno-gu · Line 3 Gyeongbokgung Station Exit 5
Getting there: Myeongdong Stn (Line 4) → Chungmuro (transfer Line 3) → Gyeongbokgung (~20 min, ₩1,450).
Opens 9am. Guard changing ceremony at 10am — highly recommended, worth timing. ₩3,000 entry. Allow 1–1.5h.
Sidequest: Rent a hanbok (traditional dress) for ₩15,000 from any of the rental shops on the east side — entry is FREE with hanbok and you'll look great in photos.
10:30 — Bukchon Hanok Village
📍 Gye-dong, Jongno-gu · 15 min walk northeast from palace
600+ traditional Korean hanok houses on winding hillside alleys. One of the most photographed streetscapes in Asia. Free, no entry. Most beautiful in the morning light. Walk north from the palace along Bukchon-ro.
Sidequest: Insadong (10 min walk south) — artisan district, pottery, calligraphy, Korean tea houses, Ssamziegil courtyard. Good for a 20-min browse if time allows.
12:00 — Gwangjang Market · Last Seoul Meal
📍 88 Changgyeonggung-ro, Jongno-gu · Jongno 5-ga Station Exit 8
Korea's oldest continuously operating market (1905). Go straight to the indoor food hall. Get: bindaetteok (mung bean pancakes, fried tableside), mayak gimbap (tiny addictive rice rolls), yukhoe (raw beef tartare). Cash only. ₩10,000–20,000.
⚠ Leave Gwangjang by 12:45 max.

📍 18 Marina Gardens Drive · MRT to Bayfront (CE/DT lines) · 5 min walk

12:30 — Lau Pa Sat + Satay Street · Lunch
📍 18 Raffles Quay, CBD · 5 min walk from Raffles Place MRT
Historic 1894 Victorian cast-iron market hall in the CBD. Satay Street (Boon Tat St, outside the hall) opens at noon — 100m of charcoal-grilled satay stalls with peanut sauce and ketupat rice cakes. Get hokkien mee, rojak, or laksa inside. End with chendol (coconut milk shaved ice). ~SGD 20–25.

📋 Itinerary — May 25 (Mon) · Full Day · Leave by 15:30
08:30 — Breakfast · Kacang Pool
📍 Restoran Kacang Pool Haji, Johor Bahru
JB's most iconic dish — kacang pool: spiced fava beans cooked in a thick tomato-herb sauce, served with thick toasted bread. You will not find this dish anywhere else in the world outside of Johor. ~RM 8. Eat on the street or inside. Start the morning right.
09:30 — Jalan Tan Hiok Nee · Heritage Street
📍 Jalan Tan Hiok Nee, JB · 5 min Grab from hotel or 20 min walk
JB's most charming heritage street — art deco shophouses, street art murals, indie cafes, clothing boutiques. Go to the banana cake bakery at #13 (opens 9am, often sells out by 11am). Grab a cup of kopi from any kopitiam. Walk the full length (~300m) and back.
Sidequest: Jalan Dhoby (parallel street) — Melbourne-inspired specialty coffee shops, quiet morning vibe. Good if you want a proper flat white.
10:30 — Johor Old Chinese Temple + Sultan Abu Bakar Mosque
📍 Johor Old Chinese Temple ↗ · 📍 Sultan Abu Bakar Mosque ↗
Both within 10 min walk of Jalan Tan Hiok Nee. The mosque overlooks the Straits of Johor — one of the most beautiful colonial mosques in Malaysia. Free to visit outside of prayer times. Good photos.

Munich's famous open-air market. Get the Weisswurst breakfast — white veal sausages (you suck the meat out of the skin with your mouth, don't cut it), sweet Bavarian mustard, a pretzel, and a Weissbier (morning beer is culturally correct here — Weisswurst must be eaten before noon). ~€12–15. 📍 Sendlinger Str. 32 · 5 min walk from Marienplatz
One of Europe's most extraordinary small churches — the Asam brothers built it in 1746, crammed with ornate frescoes, gold stucco, and a gilded high altar in a tiny space. Free entry, 15 min. Almost no tourists find this. Don't skip it.

Former royal palace of the Bavarian monarchs. 130 rooms spanning Renaissance, Baroque, Rococo and Neoclassical. One of the most significant royal collections in Europe. Opens 9am. ~€9. Allow 45–60 min.
